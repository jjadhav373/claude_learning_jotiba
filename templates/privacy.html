Privacy Policy

Spendly is built to help you understand your spending while treating your personal information with care.

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Data We Collect How We Use Your Data Data Storage Third Party Services Contact Us

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we may collect your name, email address, and login credentials. When you use the app, we collect the expense details you choose to enter, such as amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

We may also collect basic technical information, such as browser type and error details, to keep the service reliable and secure.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information to provide Spendly's core features, including saving expenses, creating summaries, and showing spending patterns. We also use it to authenticate accounts, respond to support requests, and improve the app.

We do not sell your personal information or use your expense history for targeted advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense information is stored on systems used to operate Spendly. We take reasonable measures to protect it from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. You should use a strong, unique password and contact us promptly if you believe your account has been accessed without permission.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on trusted third-party providers for services such as hosting, authentication, error monitoring, or email delivery. These providers may process limited information only as needed to perform services for us and are expected to protect that information.

Spendly does not connect to your bank accounts or access financial information outside the data you enter directly into the app.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, want to understand the data associated with your account, or would like to request its removal, please contact the Spendly team through the support channel associated with your account.
